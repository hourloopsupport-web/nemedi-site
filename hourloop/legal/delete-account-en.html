Hourloop Account Deletion

Game: Hourloop: Auto-Battler Roguelike Developer: NeMedi Last updated: 4 October 2026
Русский English

The game Hourloop: Auto-Battler Roguelike (developed by NeMedi) requires no registration: in Game versions featuring the chronite mechanics, the Game automatically creates an account with a random Player ID on the server to securely store chronites, keys, items, and Clock Tower results (if your version has no chronites, no account exists on the server). Account deletion can be performed via the methods below:

1. In the game — immediately

Works for any account, including unlinked guest profiles:

Open "Settings" → "Account" → "Delete account" → "Delete forever". The account and all of its data on the server will be erased immediately.

2. Here, without the game — immediately

For accounts linked to a VK ID or email, deletion is performed on this page:

Via VK ID
or by email
Via email confirmation code

3. By email to support — up to 30 days

  1. Copy your Player ID in the game: open the Shop tab → History subtab (at the bottom of the screen) and tap the Player ID line — the identifier will be copied to your clipboard. If you linked an email, you may write to support directly from that email address without specifying your Player ID.
  2. Send a deletion request: send an email to hourloop.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion", specifying your copied Player ID in the message body (or send from your linked email).
  3. Processing time: player support will confirm receipt; all data associated with the specified Player ID will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from the game server within 30 days.

If the game is already uninstalled and the ID was not saved

If you have already deleted the Game from your device and did not save your Player ID, account deletion depends on whether you linked your account:

  • If you linked an email: delete your account on this page using a confirmation code sent to your email, or send a deletion request to hourloop.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" directly from that linked email address without needing the Player ID.
  • If you linked a VK ID: delete your account on this page via VK ID.
  • If you did not link your account: it is impossible to locate your account. The Game collects no personal data, and without your Player ID, neither the developer nor anyone else can determine which record in the database belonged to you. Furthermore, nobody can log in to your old account either: the secret access token was stored strictly in an internal file on your device and was deleted alongside the application.

What data is deleted from the server

Upon deletion (in the game and on this page — immediately; by email request — within 30 days), the following data is permanently erased:

  • Wallet and authentication: the wallet record, balances of chronites, winding keys, and gilded keys, and the authentication token;
  • Account links: stored links to your VK user ID or email address;
  • Email confirmation codes (auth_codes): record by address fingerprint;
  • Transaction ledger: the complete history of currency credits and debits, weekly milestones, and Game+ tier confirmations;
  • Run sessions: campaign session identifiers and timestamps;
  • Banner guarantees (pity): counters of performed pulls;
  • Item inventory: all awarded equipment items and their rolled stats;
  • Pull history: records of items obtained from banner pulls;
  • Clock Tower: tower run history (endless_runs) and records in all weekly leaderboards (endless_best).

What the game server does not store

The game server never collects or stores your name, phone number, permanent device identifier (Android ID), advertising identifier (GAID/OAID), device model, or IP address, nor your email address (unless you voluntarily linked it to your account). Since this information is never retained on the server, there is nothing to delete.

What happens to other data

  • On the game server: no data associated with your Player ID remains.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics and ads: analytical gameplay events in AppMetrica and technical data collected by third-party ad networks (Yandex Mobile Ads, Petal Ads, VK Ads) are handled on their servers according to their respective policies (see sections 7 and 9 of the Privacy Policy). You can reset your device advertising identifier (GAID / OAID) at any time in your phone's privacy settings.
  • Data on your phone: deleting server data does not affect local files stored on your device. To delete local progress (talents, regular gold, settings), go to "Settings" inside the game and tap "Reset Progress", or simply uninstall the Game from your device.

Support contacts
