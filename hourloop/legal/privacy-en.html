Privacy Policy — Hourloop

Last updated: 31 August 2026
Русский English 简体中文

This policy explains what data is processed in the mobile game Hourloop (the "Game"), by whom and for what purpose. The Game is distributed through Huawei AppGallery and RuStore.

Developer: NeMedi
Studio Contact: nemedistudio@gmail.com
Game Support: hourloop.support@gmail.com

1. In short

The Game requires no registration, never asks for your name, email, phone number or payment details, and has no servers of its own that your progress would be sent to. There are no accounts in the Game, and all game progress is stored on your device only.

Two kinds of data leave your device, and neither of them identifies you personally:

  • data collected by advertising networks in order to serve ads (section 4). Ads are shown only when you tap the rewarded-video button;
  • anonymous gameplay statistics the Game sends to the AppMetrica analytics service (section 5): how far players get, where they lose most often, which upgrades they pick. This data is used to tune difficulty and balance.

2. What data is processed, and on what basis

Data Purpose Who processes it Basis
Game progress and settings retaining progress between sessions your device only no transfer takes place
Device advertising identifier (GAID / OAID) selecting more relevant ads advertising networks, AppMetrica your consent. Not sent if you declined or have not answered
Technical information: device model, OS and Game version, language, region, IP address, connection type serving ads, fraud prevention, making statistics work advertising networks, AppMetrica legitimate interest: without this information ads and statistics do not function
Gameplay events: start and end of a run, defeats, card choices analysing progression, tuning difficulty and balance AppMetrica the developer's legitimate interest in improving the Game

The Game processes no payment data: it currently has no in-app purchases.

Device permissions. The Game requires internet access and network state information; without them ads cannot load. In the Huawei AppGallery version, the Huawei ad SDK additionally declares permissions to query the list of installed apps and to install apps: these are part of that SDK and are used by the advertising system itself; the Game does not call them.

The Game never requests access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or call logs.

3. What is stored on your device

These files live in the app's internal storage, are never uploaded anywhere, and are removed together with the Game:

What Why
Game progress: in-game currency, purchased and active talents, unlocked abilities, permanent bonuses so progress survives between sessions
Recent run history: outcome, act reached, character stats, upgrades taken to show you statistics of past attempts
Music and sound volume settings, selected language so you don't have to set them again
Your answer about ad personalisation so you are not asked on every launch

The Game has no cloud saves — game progress never leaves your device. The statistics in section 5 are not game progress.

4. Advertising

The Game uses:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads — in all versions of the Game;
  • Petal Ads (Huawei Ads) — in the Huawei AppGallery version only;
  • VK Ads (formerly myTarget) — in the RuStore version only. It is connected through Yandex Mobile Ads mediation: the request goes to Yandex, which then decides whether to serve its own ad or pass the impression to VK Ads.

To select and deliver ads, and to prevent fraud, these networks may process:

  • the device advertising identifier (GAID on devices with Google services, OAID on Huawei devices) — a non-permanent identifier you can reset or limit in your device settings;
  • technical device information: model, OS version, language, region, connection type, screen resolution;
  • IP address;
  • ad delivery data: which ads were shown, whether they were watched to the end, whether they were clicked. The Yandex ad SDK reports such technical ad events to the AppMetrica analytics service (owned by Yandex) — this is part of how the ad network itself works. For the gameplay statistics the Game sends there on its own, see section 5.

The Game's developer does not receive this data and has no access to it — it goes directly to the advertising networks and is processed under their own terms; links are in section 6.

Ad personalisation is your choice. On first launch the Game asks whether the advertising networks may use your device advertising identifier to pick more relevant ads:

  • if you agree — the networks may use that data to select ads;
  • if you decline — every network is told to disable personalisation, and you see non-personalised ads;
  • until you answer — the decline setting applies.

You can change your mind at any time: Settings → the relevant-ads toggle. The networks are also given a content rating: the Game carries a 12+ rating (China 12+, Brazil A14, other regions 12+), and ads unsuitable for that age should not be shown.

5. Gameplay statistics (AppMetrica)

The Game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to the AppMetrica analytics service (owned by Yandex). This data is used to tune difficulty and balance, and to assess the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

What the service collects on its own, without the Game's involvement:

  • the fact that the Game was installed, and which ad you came from if you arrived through advertising;
  • app launches, their duration and frequency;
  • technical information: device model, OS version, Game version, language, and approximate country and region derived from your IP address;
  • crash data: technical information about where the Game terminated with an error.

What the Game sends as its own events:

  • start and end of a run: outcome, act reached, room number, duration, in-game currency earned;
  • entering and completing a room: room type, act number, health remaining, time elapsed;
  • defeat: the act, room and wave where it happened;
  • defeating an act boss;
  • picking one card out of three: the options offered and the choice made — for Parts, room rewards and permanent Legacy bonuses;
  • purchasing a node in the talent tree;
  • watching a rewarded video ad.

The statistics do not contain your name, email, phone number, any text you have typed, the contents of your device, or data about your location: location collection is disabled in the Game. Country and region are approximated by the service from your IP address.

Your device advertising identifier (GAID / OAID) is passed to AppMetrica only if you agreed to ad personalisation (see section 4). If you declined or have not answered, the identifier is not passed.

The developer has access to this data in the form of reports. It cannot be tied to a particular person: the Game holds no information identifying you.

7. How long data is kept

  • On your device — until you reset progress in Settings or uninstall the Game. No automatic retention periods apply.
  • By the advertising networks and AppMetrica — for the periods set by their own policies (links in section 6). The developer does not control those periods.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • Change your ad personalisation decision: Settings → the relevant-ads toggle. It takes effect immediately and is passed to every network, AppMetrica included.
  • Reset your advertising identifier: in your device settings, under privacy or ads. After a reset neither the ad networks nor the analytics recognise you any more.
  • Delete data from your device: Settings → reset progress permanently deletes game progress and run history. Uninstalling the app removes every file listed in section 3.
  • Ask a question or submit a request about data processing: write to the address in section 11.
  • Contact the recipients directly: rights regarding data processed by Yandex, Huawei and VK are exercised through them; the procedure is described in their policies, linked in section 6.

9. Children

The Game is not specifically directed to children: it targets a general audience and carries an official age rating of 12+ based on its content, not on its intended audience. It collects no personally identifying information. The "child-directed app" flag is not sent to the advertising networks; the 12+ content rating is sent instead (section 4).

10. Changes to this policy

If the set of services used by the Game changes, this policy is updated and the date at the top is revised. The current version is always available at this address.
